# Product

SkillBough: Build a workforce ready for tomorrow.

AI workforce development software that analyzes employee competencies against organizational requirements and recommends relevant training pathways.

- Founder and CEO: Oluwatobi Akande
- Co-founder and CTO: Fatima Abdullahi
- Industry: Human Resources, Education, Enterprise Software
- Customers: HR departments, corporate training companies and professional development providers
- Core features: Workforce skills mapping, training gap identification, personalized learning recommendations, competency tracking, workforce development analytics
- MVP: Employee assessments and manager-reviewed training recommendations
- Planned AWS: Bedrock, SageMaker AI, DynamoDB, S3, Lambda
- Business model: Per-employee subscriptions
- Main action: Book a skills audit
