How a SkillBough audit runs

  1. Set the roles and invite the team

    We load your role profiles and send each employee a 20-minute assessment.

  2. Read the gap report

    A week later the report shows each team’s skills against its roles, gap by gap.

  3. Approve the pathways

    Managers review each suggested pathway, change what they need and send it to the employee.

SkillBough’s platform on AWS

The audit platform we are building. The public demo calls none of these services.

  • S3 and Lambda

    Role profiles, course catalogues and results, scored as they arrive

  • SageMaker AI

Questions

What HR teams ask before an audit.

Ask about an audit

How long does the assessment take?

About 20 minutes per employee, on a phone or laptop.

Does it rate performance?

No. It measures skills against role needs; results never feed pay or performance ratings.

What does it cost?

A subscription per employee per month. We quote after the first audit.

Can we use our own courses?

Yes. Link your learning catalogue and SkillBough recommends from it first.

Who sees the results?

The employee, their manager and HR. You set the roles that can view each report.
